JMdict/EDICT and KANJIDIC

This site uses the JMdict/EDICT and KANJIDIC dictionary files. These files are the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, and are used under the Group's license, Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0.

Common words, their readings and many meanings match JMdict. Kanji readings and many meanings match KANJIDIC. The dictionary data was reached through the npm package kanji-data 1.1.0, which draws on kanjiapi.dev. Each month an automatic check compares the words and readings here with the latest versions of these files, and any difference is reviewed by hand.

KanjiVG
