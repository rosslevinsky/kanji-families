The short version

You can use everything here without an account. Your progress then stays in your browser and is never sent to this site. Signing in is optional, and it only lets your progress follow you between devices.

In your browser

The app keeps your progress in your browser's local storage: your answers, scores, review schedule and test results, a random ID for this browser, your test settings, and your light or dark theme choice. If you sign in, it also records a coded ID for your account, which can't be turned back into your Google account ID.

If you sign in with Google

The site asks Google only for the openid permission, which identifies an account without sharing its email address or name. The one thing kept from Google is its ID number for your account. Google can see that you signed in to this site.

Signing in sets a cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 90 days, and a second cookie that lasts up to 10 minutes while you sign in. Neither is used for advertising or tracking.

What the site stores when you sign in

  • Google's ID number for your account.
  • Your progress: scores, review schedule, your most recent 400 answers and 100 tests, and a random ID for each browser you use.
  • When your progress was first saved and last changed.

The first time you sign in, any progress already in that browser is added to your account.

Other companies involved

  • Cloudflare hosts this site and its database. Like any web host, it handles your connection, including your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on these pages, so your browser asks Google for them on every visit, whether or not you sign in.
  • Google handles the sign-in, if you use it.

The site itself has no analytics or advertising.

Signing out

Signing out first saves your latest answers to your account, then removes your progress from that browser. Your account keeps its copy.

Deleting your data

Sign in, open History, and choose “Delete my data.” This removes your saved progress from the database and from that browser right away. Your other devices remove their copies the next time they connect.

To stop those other devices from saving the deleted data again, the site keeps a short note that the account was deleted. It holds a coded version of Google's ID number, which can't be turned back into that number without the site's secret key, and the time of the deletion. Notes become removable after 90 days and are cleared the next time anyone deletes an account, so one can stay longer than that if no one does.

On the free plan this site uses, Cloudflare's database service can restore the database to any point in the last 7 days, so deleted data leaves those copies within 7 days.

Backup files

“Download backup” saves your progress as a file on your device. It is sent nowhere. It holds your progress and a random browser ID, and no account ID. Deleting your data doesn't touch files you saved, so remove those yourself if you want them gone.

If you never sign in

To remove your progress, use “Clear history” in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
