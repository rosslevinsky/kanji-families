Kanji Family Drills

Most kanji past the first few hundred are built from a meaning part and a sound part. Learn the common parts here, then drill them in Practice on a review schedule. Tap any kanji or word for details.

Roughly speaking, about 60% of the 2,136 jōyō kanji contain a sound part. For about a third of all jōyō kanji it still points to the on’yomi, and for about half the meaning part gives a useful category. These are estimates, so treat each clue as a guess to check.

Meaning components

The meaning part usually points to a category, not an exact meaning. These are the ones you will meet most often.

Meaning parts that mislead

特

Cow 牛 → “special.” It originally meant a bull. The meaning drifted; the component stayed.

館

Food 食 → “hall, building.” It started as a lodging where travelers were fed.

英

Plant 艹 → “excellent, England.” It originally meant a flower or blossom.

物

Cow 牛 → “thing.” Usually explained as a multicolored ox, which came to mean things of every kind.

頭

頁 “page” → “head.” As a component 頁 means head, not page: 頭 顔 額 head, face, forehead, and 題 title, the head of a text.

服

月 isn’t always flesh → “clothes.” In 服 (and 前) the 月 started as 舟, a boat. The two shapes merged over time.

猫

Dog 犭 → “cat.” 犭 marks animals in general: 猫 cat, 猿 monkey, and 獲 obtain, which began as catching game.

神

礻 altar is not 衤 clothing. They differ by one stroke. 礻 is worship: 神 社 祝 god, shrine, celebrate. 衤 is clothing: 補 複 被 mend, double, suffer.

都

阝 on the left is a hill; on the right it’s a town. Left: 陸 階 防 land, stairs, defend. Right: 都 部 郊 capital, section, suburbs.

経

Thread 糸 → “pass through, manage.” It first meant the warp threads on a loom. 統 govern and 約 promise also grew out of thread words.

算

Bamboo 竹 → “calculate.” Counting rods and writing slips were bamboo, hence 算 第 等 簡 calculate, order, rank, simple.

費

Shell 貝 → “spend.” Cowrie shells were early money, so 貝 appears in 費 貯 貨 賃 expense, save, goods, wages.

零

Rain 雨 → “zero.” It first meant a light, falling rain, then scraps or fractions, then zero.
